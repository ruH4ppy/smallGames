GAME IDEA

game engine: gedot probably

- probably pixel art style, but could change if needed
- top down or isometric

- Vampire survivor like
- use skills like PoE (1 skill with a few supports)
- gain xp to lvl up to gain more base damage for your skill
- maybe a dash on a 1.5 - 2 sec cd
- new monster wave every 10 - 20 sec
- boss wave every 5 - 10 waves
- 1 stage has 20 waves with an endboss on wave 20
- when stage is completed, you move to your base camp to rest/upgrade gear
- 5 stages in total for a full run probably

- The game should be more like a rougelike. Your character doesn't reset, when you clear a stage and you can upgrade your gear.
  But when you die everything is reset to zero. (I don't think I want meta progression)
- Itemization is like poe helmet, gloves, boots, body armour and jewelery
- each item has 4 affixes (2 prefixes, 2 suffixes) and can be crafted between stages
- multiple base types for different implicits
- crafting should be simple so it doesn't take alot of time to learn/time between stages
  (select item to craft -> get random selection of affixes)
- can find unique items in stages
